I built android-eval, a harness in which local decision models navigate an Android emulator over many steps to complete 30 tasks (read a value, change a setting, open a page), scored by a verifier that is independent of the model: it checks adb output or the on-screen layout on every observed state, so a success needs both the model's "done" and a passing check. In the main eval (original loop, 5 models x 5 runs, 750 episodes) the best model, d3:4b with screenshots, succeeded in 63% of episodes; four of the five models never reported done on an unfinished task, and kev did so in 4 of 150 episodes (3%). Three changes to the loop (no "stuck" option, switch state and tap effect, scroll end) raised d3:4b, clef-flash-8k, tev1:4b and kev-4b in a re-test, d3:4b to 67% in a single exp1-loop run. Fine-tuning the text-only kev-4b on 7,277 records crawled from 233 Settings and Clock screens, with the test tasks' targets and end pages held out, raised it from 10% to 53% in the original loop and from 30% to 57% in the exp1 loop, still below d3:4b in the same loops (63% and 67%). The fine-tune did not transfer to an app it never saw: on 10 Phone tasks round 1 solved 3/10 and round 2 2/10, against 2/10 for the base model and 5/10 for d3:4b.
